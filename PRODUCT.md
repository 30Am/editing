## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS), GSAP + ScrollTrigger, Framer Motion, Lenis for smooth scroll, Three.js used selectively where it adds meaning (not everywhere). Confirmed by user during interview.

## Users

Primary users are not casual visitors but decision-makers evaluating a creative partner:

- Brand leaders looking for premium creative partners.
- Founders building their personal or company brand.
- Marketing teams seeking a long-term storytelling partner (not a one-off vendor).
- Creative agencies looking for a senior freelance collaborator.

The site is deliberately not built to convert everyone — it should filter for clients who value strategy as much as aesthetics, and comfortably repel those who just want cheap volume editing.

## Product Purpose

A premium, cinematic personal site for Amlan Tripathy — Creative Strategist, Creative Director, Filmmaker, Video Editor, and Digital Marketing Consultant — that persuades visitors he understands attention, storytelling, and brand building at a strategic level, not just software execution. Success is a visitor finishing the site thinking "I want this person to shape my brand," not just "nice reel."

## Positioning

Amlan combines the analytical rigor of a former management consultant with the storytelling instincts of a cinematic editor. This is the mechanism a typical video-editor portfolio can't truthfully copy: strategy first, execution second. The site's information architecture and pacing should embody that — reasoning and business impact shown alongside craft, not craft alone.

## Brand Commitments

- Approved visual system: Primary background #F7F6F3, Secondary background #ECEAE6, Primary text #111111, Secondary text #666666. Accents — Deep Indigo #4F46E5, Film Green #7AF5C8, Muted Crimson #D94A64 — appear only during interaction, highlights, and CTAs; whitespace dominates.
- Typography: Neue Montreal, General Sans, or Satoshi. Massive editorial headlines; light weight for emotional statements, medium weight for navigation; typography itself participates in animation (word masks, letter reveals).
- Motion language is a pinned brief, not open to reinterpretation: scrambled image assembly, floating depth panels, fragment assembly, morphing layouts, typography-as-mask, clipping-path video reveals, section-to-section morphing, layered parallax, cursor magnetism, spring physics, cinematic zooms, horizontal pinned storytelling. Inspiration (feeling/pacing only, never layout or visuals) from flim.ai, dny19.com, letsplayfight.com, and a Sukumar Swain-style clear information architecture.

## Product Principles

1. Strategy before spectacle — every motion and design choice should read as intentional and reasoned, not decorative.
2. Attract fewer, better-fit clients rather than mass appeal — the site can afford to be slow, confident, and selective in pacing.
3. Never fabricate proof — placeholders stay visibly provisional until real client names, testimonials, and metrics are supplied; craft must not be used to disguise invented facts as real ones.
4. The work should speak before the words do — let imagery, motion, and pacing carry meaning ahead of copy.
5. Depth over scale — the next 1–2 years favor fewer, more meaningful collaborations over high-volume production; the site's ambition and restraint should match that.
